### Dashboard Layout & Structure
To keep the presentation highly professional and clean, I organized the visual interface into dedicated structural zones:
* Top Filter Bar: Horizontal dropdown slicers for real-time data filtering by Age, Geography, and Gender.
* Right Scorecard Panel: Quick executive metrics tracking 10K Total Customers, 20.4% Churn Rate, 5.2K Active Members, and 76.49K Average Balance.
* Central Analytics Grid: A balanced layout hosting 7 distinct charts to dissect customer behavior and risk zones.

---

### The 7 Analytical Charts Built
1. Churn % by Age Group: Identifies high-risk age brackets (with the 51-60 group showing a peak 56.2% churn).
2. Total Churn by Number of Products: Tracks customer volume against the number of bank products they hold.
3. Avg Balance by Credit Score Group: Shows capital distribution across sorted credit score ranges from 350 to 850.
4. Churn % by Geography: Displays regional drop-off rates across France, Germany, and Spain.
5. Churn % by Gender: Compares the churn behavior between male and female customer segments.
6. Churn % by Tenure: A trend line mapping exactly at which year a customer is most vulnerable to leaving.
7. Churn % by Card Type: Breaks down retention rates based on credit card ownership (Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond).
